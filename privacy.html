Video Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Video Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Tech Made Simple, The Moment and Tiko & Pip to upload and schedule the owner's own original videos and read the channels' own statistics. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool requests access to the owner's YouTube channels in order to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, schedule publishing, and read view statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API). It does not access any other Google data.

How data is stored

The sign-in refresh token and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Viewers

The tool collects no information about viewers of the channels.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
